## 5. The full-stack offer, your edge, and how to win

Sell one sentence: **"Agent-first software, built like a platform."** Voice agents are the front door; full-stack builds and scalable systems are what keep clients for years.

### The offer ladder

| Tier | What | Buyer | Entry price idea |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Agents | Voice, follow-up, booking, reactivation, reporting | Clinics, local businesses | 14-day pilot |
| Agent apps | Internal copilots, RAG over their docs, tool-using agents | Startups, SMEs | 3 to 4 week build |
| Sites with CMS | Animated Next.js sites, headless CMS, SEO and booking built in | Anyone who needs a face | Fixed fee |
| Mobile apps | React Native or Flutter apps with agent features | Startups | Fixed scope per milestone |
| Platforms | Multi-service systems on Dapr and Kafka, Kubernetes, observability | Funded startups, scale-ups | Retainer or milestone |

Your CV already shows n8n, LiveKit, Dapr, Kafka and Kubernetes, so put those on the site as capability, not decoration. Only list what you can demo or evidence.

### Your unique angle: "Engineer-led, not agency-led"

1. **Hear it before you hire it.** A live agent on the page and a phone number anyone can call.
2. **Receipts, not claims.** A public ledger of real numbers and build logs. Big firms hide behind logos; you show the work.
3. **Fixed scope, from-prices, 14-day pilot.** Most small agencies hide price; big ones need a six-week discovery.
4. **Boring infrastructure under friendly agents.** Event-driven backbone (Kafka, Dapr) so an agent never drops a call at 100x volume.
5. **You own the code.** No lock-in, handover and training included.
6. **Small agents, one job each.** The Conclave idea: a council of narrow agents beats one fragile mega-bot.

### If a big firm or a freelancer mocks you

| They say | You say (calmly, with receipts) |
| --- | --- |
| "They're a tiny new team." | "Yes. That's why the person who scopes your project is the one writing it. Hear the demo, then judge." |
| "A freelancer will do it cheaper." | "A flow works until it fails at 6pm Friday. We ship monitoring, retries, handover and weekly reports." |
| "Big agencies have certifications." | "They also have a 6-week discovery and a 6-figure floor. Our pilot is live in 14 days; the case is on /receipts." |
| "n8n or a no-code tool can do this." | "We use n8n where it fits. We build the rest as code you own when volume or compliance demands it." |
| "AI agents are hype." | "Agree, which is why we start with one job and measure it weekly." |

The best answer to mockery is a page they cannot dismiss: a live call, a real number, a public build log. Never attack by name; publish the comparison page and let buyers decide.

Flat shapes, thick ink outline, offset hard shadow, lime and violet fills, occasional hand-drawn scribble and arrow, and stickers overlapping UI mock-ups. Rule: every section gets one illustration that *explains* (call flowing to calendar, lapsed customer returning), not decorates.
